# RunRate

**Tagline:** Freelancer Pricing & Runway Tool

## 1. Target User

RunRate is designed for freelancers across different professional categories who need to decide what to charge for individual jobs while considering their personal financial stability.

### Freelancer Categories

- Writers
- Designers
- Developers
- Photographers
- Consultants
- Video/Content Creators
- Translators
- Illustrators
- Virtual Assistants
- Social Media Managers
- Other Freelancers

## 2. Problem

Freelancers often find it difficult to know how much to charge for a job while also considering their own financial situation.

They may look at what other people charge or consider the client's budget, but they may not know whether a particular rate is enough for their own financial needs.

They also need a simple way to understand how accepting a job at different rates could affect their financial runway.

## 3. Main Job

> Help a freelancer evaluate different prices for a specific job and understand how each price affects their financial runway, so they can confidently decide what to charge.

## 4. Main Journey

1. Create an account or log in.
2. Select freelancer category and currency.
3. Enter monthly expenses, current savings, and average income.
4. View current financial runway.
5. Enter new job details: work type, estimated hours, experience level, and client budget.
6. Use RunRate's built-in market-rate estimate or enter a custom market-rate range.
7. Choose whether normal average income continues as the baseline or is not assumed to continue.
8. Compare different rates and their runway impact.
9. View the minimum rate and suggested rate and decide what to charge.

## 5. V1 Features

### Account & Profile
- Sign-up/account creation.
- Login.
- Persistent freelancer profile.
- Saved financial information available when the freelancer returns.

### Financial Setup
- Freelancer category selection.
- Currency selection: NGN or USD.
- Monthly expenses input.
- Current savings input.
- Average income input.
- Current runway calculation.

### Job Pricing
- Job/work type.
- Estimated hours.
- Experience level.
- Client budget.
- Built-in estimated market-rate range.
- Option to use the built-in estimate.
- Option to enter a custom market-rate range.

### Financial Impact
- Option to keep normal average income as the baseline.
- Option to evaluate the job without assuming normal income continues.
- Runway impact of different job rates.
- Comparison of pricing scenarios.

### Pricing Recommendations
- Minimum rate.
- Suggested rate.
- Explanation of what different rates mean for runway.

## 6. NOT NOW

- Additional currencies beyond NGN and USD.
- Automatic currency conversion.
- Bank/account connections.
- Invoicing.
- Payment collection.
- Advanced expense tracking.
- Client management.
- Job/project management.
- Advanced portfolio management.
- Automatic real-time market-rate data.
- Notifications and reminders.
- Teams or collaboration.
- Other features not required for the core pricing-and-runway journey.

## 7. Constraints

- V1 supports only NGN and USD.
- The product supports multiple freelancer categories.
- Market rates are estimates, not guaranteed market prices.
- Freelancers can override the built-in market-rate estimate.
- Freelancers control the income-continuity assumption.
- Financial information must persist between sessions through the user's account.
- Authentication is required for V1.
- Calculations should be understandable to beginner users.
- V1 stays focused on pricing decisions and financial runway.

## 8. Success Condition

V1 succeeds if a freelancer can create an account, save their financial information, return later, enter a potential job, compare rates, understand each rate's runway impact, see the minimum rate, see the suggested rate, and confidently decide what to charge.

> “What should I charge for this job, and is that rate actually good enough for my financial situation?”
